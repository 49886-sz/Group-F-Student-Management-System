## Scrum Ceremonies / Meetings

### 1. Daily Stand-up (Semi-Weekly)
- **When:** Every Tuesday & Thursday, 8:00 – 9:00 PM
- **Duration:** 15 minutes (core stand-up) + 45 minutes working session
- **Who:** Scrum Master, Product Owner, Developers
- **Purpose:** Sync progress, identify blockers, plan the day's work
- **Questions answered:**
  - What did you accomplish since the last meeting?
  - What will you work on today?
  - Are there any blockers?

### 2. Sprint Planning
- **When:** First meeting of each sprint
- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Who:** Product Owner (hosts), Scrum Master, Developers
- **Purpose:** PO presents highest-priority user stories; team commits to sprint backlog

### 3. Sprint Review
- **When:** Last meeting of each sprint
- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Who:** Product Owner (hosts), Scrum Master, Developers
- **Purpose:** Team demonstrates the working increment; PO accepts or rejects

### 4. Sprint Retrospective
- **When:** After Sprint Review
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Who:** Scrum Master, Developers (PO optional)
- **Purpose:** Reflect on what went well, what to improve
